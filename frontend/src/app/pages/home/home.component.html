Three specialized tools designed to maximize agricultural productivity and crop health.

1. Crop Recommendation

Input soil nutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorous, Potassium, pH, Rainfall) and your city. The system fetches live weather metrics to predict the most profitable and high-yielding crop.

2. Fertilizer Recommendation

Upload an image of an affected leaf. The system identifies plant diseases across 38 categories with immediate treatment steps and prevention tips.

How AgriDisha Operates

A modern, resilient decoupled microservice architecture

1
Input Data

Enter soil N-P-K parameters or upload a leaf photo in the Angular frontend.

2
Spring Boot Gateway

Spring Boot validates inputs, integrates OpenWeatherMap, and secures requests.

3
FastAPI Inference

Python ML service evaluates XGBoost/Random Forest and PyTorch ResNet9 models.

4
Actionable Output

Get crystal-clear crop selections, fertilizer dosages, and plant treatment guides.
